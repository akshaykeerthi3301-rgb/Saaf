Text scores the posting you are looking at. The file is attached to résumé uploads when you press “Fill this application”.

For filling forms

Salary (only if you want it filled)

In lakhs per year. Written in the form's own unit — “18 LPA”, or rupees where the box asks for rupees. Left blank, salary boxes are left for you.

Your yes/no answers

Used only for these three questions. Left blank, they are left for you.

Apply for me

Saaf opens each queued job in a background tab, fills it from the details above, and presses Submit — only when every required answer is filled. It stops and leaves the tab for you at any CAPTCHA, any question it has no answer for, or a required file it doesn't have. Up to 25 a day. A job counts as applied only when the employer's page confirms it.

      Never filled: gender, caste, religion and other demographic questions; “why do you want to work here” and other free text; consent boxes; passwords, Aadhaar and PAN. Every dropdown or button we pick is listed on the card for you to check. Saaf presses Submit only in “Apply for me”, only after you switch it on, and never past a CAPTCHA.
