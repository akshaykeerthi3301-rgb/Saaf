Questions

Naukri already says it matches me. Why is this different?
Because we'll show you the roles we refused, and why. Open your own profile and check the first one against the job description. It takes thirty seconds.
Can my employer find out I'm looking?
No. No employer is ever contacted, nothing here is public or sold, and no recruiter can search this. Delete your account in one click, no email required.
What happens to my résumé?
It's parsed once to fill your profile, then the file is deleted. Only the fields you confirm are stored.
Why isn't LinkedIn or Naukri in here?
Their terms don't allow it. We pull from company hiring boards directly — which is why some roles you see elsewhere won't appear here, and why we tell you how many we indexed.
